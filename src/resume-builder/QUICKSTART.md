# Resume Builder - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Access the Resume Builder

Navigate to the Resume Builder page in your EchoMentor application. It's already integrated and ready to use!

### 2. Choose a Template

Select from 4 professional templates:
- **Modern Pro** - Clean and professional
- **Elegant** - Two-column sidebar design
- **Minimal** - Simple and clean
- **Creative** - Bold and colorful

### 3. Fill in Your Information

Use the tabbed navigation to fill in each section:

#### 📝 Personal Information
- Full Name *
- Professional Title *
- Email Address *
- Phone Number *
- Location *
- LinkedIn Profile (optional)
- Website/Portfolio (optional)
- Professional Summary *

#### 🎓 Education
- Click "Add Education" to add entries
- Fill in: Institution, Degree, Field, Dates, GPA, Description
- Add multiple education entries as needed

#### 💼 Work Experience
- Click "Add Experience" to add entries
- Fill in: Company, Role, Location, Dates
- Check "I currently work here" for current positions
- Describe your responsibilities and achievements

#### 💻 Skills
- Type a skill and press Enter to add
- Add at least 5-10 relevant skills
- Click the trash icon to remove skills

#### 🚀 Projects
- Click "Add Project" to showcase your work
- Include: Name, Description, Technologies, Link, Date
- Great for portfolios and side projects

#### 🏆 Certifications
- Click "Add Certification" to add credentials
- Include: Name, Issuer, Date, Credential ID, Link
- Add professional certifications and achievements

### 4. Watch the Live Preview

The right panel shows a live preview of your resume. It updates in real-time as you type!

### 5. Track Your Progress

The progress card shows:
- Completion percentage
- Checklist of completed items
- What's left to complete

### 6. Download Your Resume

Click "Download PDF" to export your resume. The print dialog will open where you can:
- Save as PDF
- Adjust page settings
- Print directly

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Writing Tips
- Use action verbs (Led, Built, Managed, Developed)
- Quantify achievements with numbers (Increased by 40%, Managed team of 10)
- Keep descriptions concise and impactful
- Tailor your resume for each job application

### Formatting Tips
- Keep your resume to 1-2 pages
- Use bullet points for easy scanning
- Maintain consistent formatting
- Proofread carefully for errors

### Content Tips
- Start with your most recent experience
- Focus on achievements, not just responsibilities
- Include relevant keywords from job descriptions
- Show impact and results

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### My data disappeared!
- Data is saved in your browser's localStorage
- Clearing browser data will remove saved resumes
- Use "Download PDF" to keep a backup

### Preview not updating?
- Make sure you've filled in required fields (marked with *)
- Try switching templates to refresh the preview

### Can't download PDF?
- The download uses your browser's print function
- Make sure pop-ups are not blocked
- Try a different browser if issues persist
